# Original royal celebration scenery

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool for this invitation, then encoded as responsive WebP assets. No stock photography or external image hosting is used. These are fictional environments matching the demonstration venues.

Final files in this directory:

- `welcome.webp` and `welcome-small.webp` — sunset terrace soirée.
- `ceremony.webp` and `ceremony-small.webp` — white-rose wedding aisle.
- `moonlight.webp` and `moonlight-small.webp` — chandelier-lit courtyard ball.

Full assets are 1659 × 948; smaller variants are 900 × 514. The three smaller images total approximately 313 KB. The three full images total approximately 1 MB.
